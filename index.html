Culture & Wellbeing

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Social and Emotional Wellbeing recognises that wellbeing is connected to body, mind and emotions, family, community, culture, Country and spirituality.

Traditional strengths can include:

  • Yarning
  • Connection to Country
  • Cultural identity
  • Storytelling
  • Connection with Elders
  • Community activities
  • Art and creative expression
