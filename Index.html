Your Wellbeing Matters

🧠 Mental Health

Everyone has mental health just like everyone has physical health. It's okay to ask for help if you're feeling overwhelmed, stressed, anxious, sad, or disconnected.

🌿 Culture & Connection

Connection to family, community, Country, culture, language and identity can support wellbeing, resilience and belonging.

💬 Looking After Yourself

Healthy sleep, exercise, friendships, hobbies and talking to trusted adults all contribute to positive mental health.

Substance Use Education

Drugs, alcohol and vaping can affect developing brains, learning, sport performance, relationships and mental health.

  • Know the facts before making decisions.
  • Talk to trusted adults and health professionals.
  • Learn how substances can affect mental wellbeing.
  • Support friends by encouraging healthy help-seeking.
